Sharing this guide with your students

One routine for students, in the same words you use in your syllabus and assignment directions.

What the guide does

The guide gives students one routine for using AI in any course: check the AI use label, ask the 3 Qs, and keep a trail. The guide never sets the rules. Every page sends students back to your AI Course Policy and your assignment directions.

Students in FYE 100 learn the same routine in Lesson 1.4 of the Pathfinder Guidebook (opens in a new tab), in the same words.

Add the guide to your course

  1. In your Start Here module in Blackboard, add a link to the guide's home page, next to your AI Course Policy. Students can read the home page in under a minute.
  2. Link to one page from assignment directions when that moment matters most. For example: "Before you use any AI output in this section, see Before You Use AI's Answer."
  3. Link to the live guide instead of copying the text into your course. Your course will always show the current wording.

Use the same words

Students learn the routine faster when the guide, the syllabus, and the assignment directions all use the same terms.

Shared terms used in the guide
TermWhat students see
AI use labelNo AI, AI-supported, or AI-integrated, at the top of each assignment's directions
AI Course PolicyYour syllabus section on how AI fits your course
Academic IntegrityYour syllabus section on violations and what happens
The 3 QsBefore you ask AI: Should I use AI for this part of my assignment?
Before you use AI's answer: How would I know if AI's answer is wrong?
Before you turn in your work: Can I explain my work and how AI helped me?
Keep a trailWord saved to OneDrive, a OneNote notebook for each course, and a written record of AI use
