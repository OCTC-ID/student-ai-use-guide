Student AI Use Guide

Using AI in Your Coursework

Your instructor's AI Course Policy has the rules for this course. Use this page each time you use AI for your coursework.

First, check the AI use label

Each assignment has one of three AI use labels:

  • No AI
  • AI-supported
  • AI-integrated

You can find the label in the assignment directions. If you are not sure, check the AI Course Policy or ask your instructor.

The 3 Qs

Ask yourself these three questions each time you use AI for an assignment. Each question goes with a different moment in your work. Together, they help you make good choices about AI and be ready to explain those choices.

Keep a trail

Keeping a trail means saving a record of your work as you go: your notes, your drafts, and how you used AI. Your trail shows how your work grew over time, from first ideas to final draft.

If your instructor asks how you did an assignment, you can show them. Tools that try to spot AI writing are sometimes wrong, and your trail is real evidence of your own work. With your trail, you can also tell your instructor exactly how AI helped you.

Three ways to keep a trail

  • Write in Word, saved to OneDrive

    Sign in to Word with your college account and save your file to OneDrive before you start writing. Word saves your changes as you type and keeps a version history with dates and times.

    Type your work in the document. Don't write somewhere else and paste finished text in at the end. To see your history, go to File, then Info, then Version History.

  • Keep a OneNote notebook for this course

    Open OneNote with your college account and make one notebook for this course, with a section for each assignment. OneNote adds a date to every page.

    As you work, save your notes, links to your sources, and your ideas. You can also add photos of handwritten brainstorming or whiteboard work.

  • Write down how you use AI

    When you use Copilot or another AI tool, paste your question and the part of AI's answer you used into the assignment's OneNote section.

    Add a short note about what you kept, changed, or left out, and why. This note is the start of what you tell your instructor about your AI use.
