Using AI in Your Coursework

Your instructor's AI Course Policy has the rules for this course. Use this page each time you use AI for your coursework.

First, check the AI use label

Each assignment has one of three AI use labels:

  • No AI
  • AI-supported
  • AI-integrated

You can find the label in the assignment directions. If you are not sure, check the AI Course Policy or ask your instructor.

The 3 Qs

  1. Before you ask Should I use AI for this part of my assignment? More on this (opens in a new tab)
  2. Before you use it How would I know if AI's answer is wrong? More on this (opens in a new tab)
  3. Before you hand it in Can I explain my work and how AI helped me? More on this (opens in a new tab)

Keep a trail

  • Write in Word, saved to OneDrive. Word saves a dated history of your draft while you write. Type your work in the document. Don't write it somewhere else and paste it in at the end.
  • Keep a OneNote notebook for this course. Make one section for each assignment. Save your notes, sources, and ideas there while you work.
  • Write down how you use AI. When you use Copilot or another AI tool, copy your question into OneNote. Add a note about what you kept, changed, or left out.

Your trail shows how your work grew over time. It also helps you tell your instructor how AI helped you. How to set it up (opens in a new tab)

Never paste into AI: other people's personal information, passwords, or anything private.

When AI is optional, choosing not to use it is a good choice too.
